# Asset sources

## App artwork

App icons and screenshots are local copies of the official Google Play listings provided for this portfolio:

- SmartScan: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.supraim.smartscan
- Attentio: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.supraim.attentio
- WorldRadio: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.supraim.worldradio

The WorldRadio feature carousel uses three screenshots from that listing.

## Technology logos

- Kotlin, React, Angular, C, C++, and .NET: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), downloaded from `cdn.simpleicons.org`.
- Java and C#: [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon), downloaded from jsDelivr.
- Jakarta EE: [Eclipse Foundation artwork](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jakarta_ee_logo_schooner_color_horizontal_default.svg), sourced from the Eclipse Foundation artwork archive.
- React Native uses the React mark alongside its full technology name.

Technology marks are used to identify the tools listed in the portfolio. Product names and marks remain the property of their respective owners.

## Interface icons

- GitHub and Google Play: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), downloaded from `cdn.simpleicons.org`.
- LinkedIn: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), downloaded from jsDelivr.

These marks identify outbound profile and store links. Product names and marks remain the property of their respective owners.
